One Hind, one map. Godot 4.7. Design lock: [MODE_BIBLE.md](MODE_BIBLE.md).

Headless rule check:

```
~/bin/godot --headless --path /home/hierophant/Projects/hind-merc/game -- --probe
```

Play, when you want it on this screen:

```
~/bin/godot --path /home/hierophant/Projects/hind-merc/game
```

W moves forward. A moves left. S moves back. D moves right. Q turns left. E turns right. R goes up. F goes down. Left click fires. The mouse stays free.

The attack wash is on at the ground, while the Hind is still. One step up is wind. Two steps up is off. A jeep or a technical can flip. A truck shakes. A vehicle does not move sideways. The gun destroys a vehicle. Fuel at zero lands. Health at zero crashes.
